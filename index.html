    Buy me a coffee

    Toll PH is a free, one-person project with no ads and no sign-ups. If it saved you a wrong turn or a surprise at the toll plaza, a coffee helps keep the rates checked and the app growing.

    Completely optional. The app works exactly the same either way.

    Send feedback

    Found a wrong toll, a bug, or have an idea? Tell me here. Every message is read.

    GitHub opens with your message filled in; press Create there to send it. It needs a free GitHub account, and posts there are public, so leave out your plate number, RFID account and phone number.

    What's new

    Fees charged at the plaza prevail. Each charge is paid separately from your RFID balance.
